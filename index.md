# Alessandro Quets

Digital Designer & Frontend Developer

I care about how an interface looks, how it feels, and how it works.

I shape interactions, build design systems, and carry those decisions into production Working in both Figma and code lets me keep refining the details as I build.

I can sit in a design review and open a pull request on the same day.

My background is in communication and information design. Over the past three years, I’ve worked with startups in DeFi and crypto, designing interfaces and building the frontend behind them.

My work has ranged from trading products and design systems to a blockchain rebrand. Across these projects, I’ve been interested in the same challenge: helping people understand and use complex products.

Alongside product work, I’m a teaching assistant in interaction design at Politecnico di Milano.
